About Rotary Pizza + Coffee

A casual Lake Havasu table for pizza, pasta, breakfast, coffee, salads and an easy bite after a day on the water.

The approved Rotary menu brings Italian-American comfort together with espresso drinks, dirty sodas, cocktails and a full collection of pizzas.
